About MonAra

Our Story

Delivering excellence in construction and technical services since 2009.

MonAra Trading Contracting & Services W.L.L. was established in Doha, Qatar with a clear vision: to provide integrated, high-quality construction, trading, and maintenance services that meet the demands of a rapidly developing nation.

Over the past decade, we have grown from a specialised contractor into a full-service provider, delivering projects across commercial, residential, industrial, and infrastructure sectors. Our multidisciplinary team combines local expertise with international best practices.

We are committed to safety, sustainability, and long-term client partnerships — delivering every project on time, on budget, and to the highest quality standards.
